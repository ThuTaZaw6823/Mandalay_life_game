Upgraded 3D open world with better visuals, improved character riding pose, upgraded wildlife, vehicles, day/night, weather and mobile-friendly control support.

Desktop: WASD · Shift · Space · E · F

Mobile: Use the joystick and touch buttons

Controls & Help

Movement

  • W / A / S / D — Walk or drive
  • Shift — Sprint / vehicle boost
  • Space — Jump / gain altitude
  • Mouse drag — Rotate camera
  • Wheel — Zoom camera

Actions

  • E — Enter / exit / interact
  • F — Pick up / complete delivery
  • C — Change avatar outfit
  • V — Change weather
  • B — Buy land
  • H — Build house
  • T — Plant tree

Interior / Roof

  • E — Go upstairs / confirm entry
  • Q — Go downstairs
  • R — Exit rooftop / stairwell shortcut

Mobile

  • Left joystick — Move
  • Camera pad — Orbit view
  • Action buttons — Jump / Interact / Sprint / Task
