FIELD GUIDE

Explore your way.

On foot: WASD moves, Shift runs, Space jumps. In vehicles: W/S drive, A/D turn. In plane: Space climbs and Shift descends. Drag to orbit the camera, scroll to zoom. E opens nearby doors or enters/exits vehicles. Inside a tall building, go to the staircase and press E to climb or Q to descend. V switches weather between Auto, Sun, Rain, Storm and Snow. Helicopters land on rooftop pads; use E to get out and R to return to street level.
